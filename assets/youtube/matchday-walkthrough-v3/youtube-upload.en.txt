TITLE
PitchTrace 3D | From Camera Setup to 3D Football Replay

DESCRIPTION
How do eight fixed views of the pitch become one football replay? Follow the crew as they check the pitch from touchline to touchline, synchronize the cameras and align frames by exposure time on PitchTrace Edge. Watch three parallel analyses turn the same sequence into player positions and identities, player actions, and the ball’s 3D path.

Then try the working browser viewer. Freeze the play, follow number nine from above, and switch archived camera recordings at the same match time.

Try the interactive demo
https://winixi.github.io/pitchtrace-3d/

Project and local setup
https://github.com/winixi/pitchtrace-3d

The eight-camera coverage and Edge processing sequences illustrate the planned design. The final minute shows the working local gateway, a built-in simulated match and a two-camera test archive. The video has English narration and on-screen English and Simplified Chinese subtitles.
