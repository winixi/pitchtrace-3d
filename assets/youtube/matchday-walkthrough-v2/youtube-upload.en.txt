TITLE
PitchTrace 3D Matchday Walkthrough | From Camera Setup to 3D Replay

DESCRIPTION
What does it take to turn a football match into a 3D replay you can explore? Follow the pitch-side crew from the first camera on its fixed mount to the opening whistle. Then take the viewer’s seat, freeze the play and follow number nine as he runs onto the ball.

Try the browser demo
https://winixi.github.io/pitchtrace-3d/

Project and local setup
https://github.com/winixi/pitchtrace-3d

The field diagrams show the planned setup. Four cameras stay fixed around the pitch for the whole match. Viewers can move a virtual camera in the 3D replay. The video has English narration and on-screen English and Simplified Chinese captions. The screen recordings show the working local gateway, the built-in simulated match and a two-camera test archive. The timing report and footage review use generated test recordings.
